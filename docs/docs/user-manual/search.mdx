# System-Wide Search

System-wide search finds persons, families, groups and reports from one search box.

## Where to search

- **Home page**: the search box at the top.
- **Any other page**: the small search box in the header, next to the Jethro logo.
- **Search results page**: the *System-Wide Search* box at the top of the results. Results update
  as you type, so you can refine a search without pressing Enter.

When you type into the search results page, results start appearing once you have typed **two
characters**. Clearing the box clears the results. The address bar keeps up with what you type, so
reloading the page or pressing Back keeps your search.

**Pressing Enter** runs the search as a normal page load. If it finds exactly one thing, Jethro
goes straight to that person, family, group or report instead of showing a list. Typing
without pressing Enter never leaves the results page.

## Search for...

Open **Search for...** under the search box to choose what kind of record to find:

| Choice | Finds |
|---|---|
| everything | all of the below |
| families | families |
| persons | persons |
| groups | groups |
| reports | reports that are shared, or that you own |

## Search in...

Open **Search in...** to choose which information to look in. *Everything* looks in all of them.

| Choice | Looks in | How it matches | Finds |
|---|---|---|---|
| everything | all of the below | as below | everything |
| name | person first, last and full name; family name; group and report names | start of any word | persons, families, groups, reports |
| phone | mobile and work phone (persons), home phone (families) | the whole number exactly | persons, families |
| email | email address | the whole address exactly | persons |
| address | family street, suburb and postcode | start of any word | families |
| contact remarks | a person's contact remarks | anywhere in the text | persons |
| *a custom field* | that custom field | start of any word | persons |

**How matching works:**

- **Start of any word**: `hill` finds "12 Hill St" and "Hillcrest Rd", but not "Churchill Ave".
  `21` finds postcodes 2150 and 2155. Capitals don't matter.
- **Anywhere in the text**: `diabet` finds "pre-diabetic" and "Diabetic, carries insulin".
- **Exactly**: a phone number must be complete. Spaces and other punctuation are ignored, so
  `0491 570 157` finds `0491570157`, and a number starting with the international prefix (e.g.
  `+61`) is matched in its local form. A partial number such as `0491` finds no phone numbers.
- With *everything*, a search made of digits is first tried as a phone number. Only if no person or
  family has that exact number are names and the other information searched.

Only custom fields marked **Include in system-wide search** (in *Admin → Custom Fields*) are
searched, and each one appears as its own *Search in* choice. Only text custom fields can be marked
this way.

Some combinations can never match, such as *Search for: groups* with *Search in: address*. These
simply show "No results were found".

## Results

Results are listed in this order:

1. Groups and reports whose names match.
2. **Name matches**: persons and families whose names match. A family whose name matches is shown
   with its matching members indented beneath it.
3. **Persons that matched on other information**: phone, email, contact remarks or a custom field.
4. **Families that matched on other information**: phone or address.

Archived persons, families and groups are shown greyed out, after all active ones.

When a person or family was found by something other than its name, the line below its name shows
which information matched and the matching text, for example:

> Torrie Lockman<br />
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Friend Of: **Torrie***

Click anywhere on a result row, or its **View** link, to open it.

## What you can see

Search respects your account's restrictions. If your account is restricted to certain
congregations or groups, you only see persons and families you are allowed to see.

## Other search boxes

The search boxes on the **Persons**, **Families** and **Groups** lists, and the person picker used
in forms, are separate. They search names (and, for persons, searchable custom fields) only.
