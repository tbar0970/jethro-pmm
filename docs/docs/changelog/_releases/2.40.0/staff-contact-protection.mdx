### Staff contact protection in the members area

Email addresses and mobile numbers for anyone with a control centre account can
only be changed through the control centre, not through the members area.
This applies to self and family-member edits, including inactive accounts and
accounts without mandatory two-factor authentication. Protected contacts remain
visible as read-only inputs with a security explanation on hover. Other existing
member-area editing permissions are unchanged.
