### Member directory: choose what you share

Sharing your details with a group is now a per-person choice instead of an
all-or-nothing group setting ([#792](https://github.com/tbar0970/jethro-pmm/issues/792)).
A new **Sharing** page in the members area lists your groups, each with
**Share my Name** (your name appears in the directory) and **Share my Contact
Info** (your email and phone are shown alongside it) columns, plus a default row that
applies to sharing-enabled groups where you haven't chosen individually.
Each column either records your choice or follows your default: an inherited
column is read-only and shows your default's current setting, updating as soon
as you change the default. Sharing your contact info also shares your name.
Each column shows "set directly | use default" (or "uses default" while
inheriting): the **use default** link returns it to following your default, and
**set directly** lets you override it for one group. Staff can record a member's consent on the group's member list
("Edit sharing settings"), and each person record has a **Sharing** tab
showing which sharing-enabled groups the person currently consents to, since
when, and whether consent was recorded by the member or by staff - staff can
edit it there directly, and members can opt out of individual groups even when
their default is on.

Groups keep their "Members may share their contact info?" switch, which now means
*members may choose to share* rather than *everyone shares*. Existing groups
keep working: on upgrade, every current member of a sharing-enabled group is
counted as having consented, so directories don't change until individuals opt
out; these carried-over grants show as "migrated from the old group setting". Archiving a group now also stops its directory sharing, and the group
pages in the members area are visible only to group members.
